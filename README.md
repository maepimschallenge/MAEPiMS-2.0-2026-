MAEPiMS 2.0 (2026)
Mathematics | Epidemiology | Machine Learning | Social Indicators

A multidisciplinary, pan-African competition designed for the next generation of thinkers to apply their skills to solve real-world challenges.

This initiative focuses on the surveillance, prediction, and analysis of infectious diseases by integrating mathematical modelling, machine learning, and socioeconomic indicators.

**100% Free to Enter | Powered by The Adejimi Adeniji Foundation**

##Challenge / Purpose
To task participants with developing actionable solutions and policy recommendations for public health and societal impact across Africa.

## Aims
- Equipping the next generation of thinkers with challenging tasks to enhance their skills for academic and community impact.
- Utilizing mathematical modelling and machine learning to provide actionable solutions and recommendations for policymakers.

## Objectives
- To foster interdisciplinary research between mathematics and other fields.
- The integration and development of machine learning algorithms and mathematical modelling for research focused on infectious disease, social modelling, prediction, and interventions.

## What's New in the 2nd Edition
- **Pan-African scope** — open to researchers across the entire continent.
- **Three Award Categories** — Team, Individual, and a brand-new **Female Category** dedicated to celebrating and empowering women in STEM.
- **Publication pathway** — outstanding submissions (top three per category) will be supported through to publication in high-impact, peer-reviewed journals, with participants credited as co-authors.

## Award Categories
- **Team Category** — teams of 3–4 competing for the grand prize.
- **Individual Category** — compete independently and showcase your analytical brilliance.
- **Female Category** — a dedicated track recognising outstanding women researchers, including mentorship and scholarship opportunities.

## Why Participate?
- Apply your skills to tackle real-world challenges in public health.
- Work on cutting-edge problems in disease surveillance and prediction.
- Collaborate with peers and gain recognition for innovative solutions.
- Get your work published in peer-reviewed journals — a genuine career launchpad.
- Make a meaningful contribution to public health and resilience across Africa.

## Submission Format
All submissions must be compiled into a **single PDF document** including all required sections as specified in the competition guidelines:

`Title` · `Abstract` · `Introduction` · `Assumptions` · `Forecasting Methodology` · `Model Architecture` · `Model Justification` · `Results` · `Policy Implications` · `Discussion` · `Uncertainty Analysis` · `Conclusions` · `References`

All accompanying code must be **uploaded to GitHub**, and the **repository link included within the submitted PDF**.
